Create a private league, invite your group, build a roster, schedule matches, and keep results and player statistics organized throughout the season.

Follow each matchweek
- Upcoming games and saved lineups
- Live match state and final scores
- Competition tables and match history
- Player appearances, goals, assists, clean sheets, and form

Play together
- Pick a five-player Fantasy team from valid match lineups
- Choose a captain and compete in your league standings
- Make friendly predictions and review your personal history
- Receive optional matchday, Fantasy, result, and league notifications

Made for private football groups
- Create or join multiple leagues
- Switch leagues without mixing their data
- Invite friends with secure links or approval-based league codes
- Give owners and admins focused controls for rosters, fixtures, lineups, results, seasons, and members

Predictions use virtual league coins for entertainment only. Coins cannot be purchased, sold, withdrawn, redeemed for prizes, or exchanged for money. Thursday League does not offer real-money gambling or financial services.

An account and internet connection are required. Fantasy and prediction features can be disabled by a league owner. Predictions unlock after three completed matches when enabled.
